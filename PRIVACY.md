# Privacy Policy

**Minimal for YouTube** does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal
data.

## What the extension stores

Your own settings, and nothing else:

- whether the extension is on or off;
- the chosen theme (device, light or dark);
- whether Shorts are shown;
- the focus timer's length and, while it runs, the moment it ends.

These values are kept locally through the browser's `storage.local` API. They
stay on your device, are never uploaded anywhere, and are removed when you
uninstall the extension.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
- No tracking of what you watch, search for or click.
- No accounts, no registration, no identifiers.
- No servers of our own — the extension never makes a network request.
- No remote code: every script it runs ships inside the package.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| permission | purpose |
|---|---|
| `storage` | keeping the settings listed above |
| `alarms` | firing the focus timer while the popup is closed |
| `notifications` | one system notification when the timer ends |
| access to `www.youtube.com` | restyling and rearranging YouTube's own pages |

Page content is never read, stored or transmitted. The extension only changes
how the page looks and where its elements are placed.

## Third-party links

The extension's popup contains links to Boosty and Ko-fi. Opening them takes
you to those services, each with its own privacy policy. Nothing about you is
passed along in the link.
